## 0.2.0 - New Features!
- Date: 2026-06-17
- Type: Major
- Authors: Matathias

Expands the scope of the mod and adds more levers to change how it plays, as well as a couple of bugfixes.

### Changes
- You can now send animals to settlements one at a time, instead of having to drop off a breeding pair together
- Animals can be sent to settlements by transport pod
- Fish integration: with Odyssey active, settlements will have access to fish depending on their biome and water access
- Added Codex entries
- Renamed mod (Pens & Pastures -> Herds & Fisheries)
