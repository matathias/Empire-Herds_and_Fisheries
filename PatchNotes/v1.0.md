## 1.0.2 - 300 Phantom Tortoises update
- Date: 2026-07-11
- Type: Patch
- Authors: Matathias

A tandem update with Empire Refactored to address an issue where 300 tortoises suddenly spawned on a player's map.

### Changes
- Block fertilized eggs from being produced by Empire settlements or trader caravans. This should prevent the possibility of stacks of fertilized eggs getting dropped on the map and hatching into a billion lions
